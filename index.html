SaaS · rental paperwork

RentLetter

Software for rental realtors in Ontario and BC — the paperwork end of leasing, made routine.

rentletter.ca ↗
Why I built it
A real market gap in Canadian rental screening — chosen from workflow and market logic, not personal taste.
Who it’s for
Realtors, landlords and ordinary tenants — a general-public audience with no patience for software. Because of that, ease of use drove nearly every design decision: the product had to be obvious on first contact to people who did not want to learn a tool.

02 / Logic

Personal tooling · markets

easymonee

A stock research and monitoring dashboard with an AI research chat at the centre of it.

easymonee.com ↗
Why I built it
Purely for myself. No market research, no customer — I wanted a specific instrument for my own investing, and nothing on the market was it. The name is deliberately ridiculous for exactly that reason: it was never meant to be sold to anyone, so it never needed a serious one. Knowing which game you are playing — building for a market or building for yourself — is half of product judgement.

One user. Me.
